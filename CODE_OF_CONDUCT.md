# Code of Conduct

**English** · [简体中文](#行为准则简体中文)

## Our standards

Participation in Local Tasks Bridge should be respectful, constructive, and
focused on helping people use and improve the software safely. Examples of
positive behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- respecting different levels of experience and different languages — English
  and Chinese are both welcome;
- protecting user privacy and redacting sensitive data;
- accepting maintainer decisions about safety and project scope.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing other
people's private information, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For a private conduct report, use the repository's private reporting form at
<https://github.com/siyuanj/local-tasks-bridge/security/advisories/new> and
begin the title with `Code of conduct report`. Don't include credentials, user
content, or unrelated personal information.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions, and in other public spaces where someone represents this project.

## 行为准则（简体中文）

### 我们的准则

参与 Local Tasks Bridge 时，请保持尊重、富有建设性，并专注于帮助大家安全地使用和改进这个软件。积极的行为包括：

- 提供可操作的技术反馈；
- 尊重不同的经验水平和不同的语言——英文和中文都同样欢迎；
- 保护用户隐私，对敏感数据打码；
- 接受维护者在安全和项目范围方面的决定。

骚扰、歧视性言论、人身攻击、公开他人的私人信息以及蓄意捣乱，都是不可接受的。

### 执行

如需私下报告违反行为准则的情况，请使用本仓库的私密报告表单 <https://github.com/siyuanj/local-tasks-bridge/security/advisories/new>，并让标题以 `Code of conduct report` 开头。请不要附上凭据、用户内容或无关的个人信息。

当有不当、威胁性或有害的行为时，维护者可以编辑或删除相关内容，并可以暂时或永久限制相关人员参与。

### 适用范围

本准则适用于本仓库的问题、拉取请求、代码审查和讨论，以及有人代表本项目出现的其他公共场合。
